# Code of Conduct

EnvTrail welcomes contributors and users of different backgrounds and experience levels. Help make project spaces useful, respectful, and safe for participation.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with care. Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent while taking the impact of your words seriously.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwelcome behavior.
- Make room for questions and different levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information or use project spaces for personal attacks.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **contact@v1sion.xyz**. Include the relevant context and links where safe. Do not post private information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review concerns in context and may edit or remove content, close or lock a discussion, restrict participation, or ban a participant. They will aim to protect the privacy of people involved while addressing the reported behavior. A report does not guarantee a particular response or timeline.

This policy applies in this repository and its project-managed spaces, and when someone represents EnvTrail publicly. Maintainers are also expected to follow it.
